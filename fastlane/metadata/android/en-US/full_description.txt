Travel Stories is an interactive guide to Incredible India.

• Explore all 36 states and union territories on rich interactive maps
• Drill into districts, tourist spots, distances, and nearby stays
• Browse curated tour packages from verified travel agents and send inquiries
• Works fully online with live data from trvlstory.reddevils.co.in

Free, no account needed. Open source (MIT) at
github.com/souravbrock/Travel_Stories_Bolt.
